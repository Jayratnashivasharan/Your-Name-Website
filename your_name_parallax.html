Your Name

君の名は。

A Story Beyond Time

In the tranquil countryside and bustling Tokyo, two souls unknowingly connect across time and space. Mitsuha, a high school girl yearning for life in the city, and Taki, a Tokyo boy consumed by his daily routine, begin to mysteriously swap bodies. As they navigate each other's lives, they discover a connection that transcends the boundaries of reality, leading them on an unforgettable journey to find one another.

Themes & Beauty

🔗

Connection

The red thread of fate binds two souls together, demonstrating that true connections transcend time, space, and memory itself.

⏰

Time

Through the interweaving of past and present, the story explores how our actions ripple through time, affecting both our future and past.

💭

Memory

Even when memories fade, the heart remembers. Some feelings persist beyond the constraints of time and consciousness.

🌅

Beauty

From twilight skies to falling stars, the film captures the breathtaking beauty of fleeting moments that define our existence.

💫

Longing

The aching desire to find someone you've never met, yet somehow always known, drives the heart of this timeless tale.

⭐

Destiny

Some encounters are written in the stars, proving that fate works in mysterious ways to bring two souls together.
